Common Causes We See Across South Jersey & Philadelphia

Burst & Frozen Pipes

Good To Know

Understanding Water Damage Categories

The restoration industry groups water damage into three categories based on contamination level — it affects how a loss should be handled.

Category 1

Clean Water

From a clean source like a supply line. Lowest risk, but still needs fast extraction and drying to prevent it from degrading.

Category 2

Gray Water

Contains some contamination — think washing machine or dishwasher overflow. Requires more careful handling and cleaning.

Category 3

Black Water

Grossly contaminated, such as sewage backups or floodwater. Requires protective equipment and thorough sanitizing.
