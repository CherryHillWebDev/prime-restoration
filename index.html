Shut off the water main and power to any affected circuits first, then photograph every affected area before you start cleanup — documentation matters for your insurance claim. Move valuables to a dry area if it's safe to do so, and never wade into standing water near outlets or appliances. Most standard homeowners policies in New Jersey and Pennsylvania cover sudden, accidental damage — a burst pipe, storm wind, or fire — but they typically exclude gradual leaks and rising floodwater, which usually requires separate flood coverage. Every policy is different, so check your declarations page or call your agent to confirm. We can help document the damage in a way that supports your claim, but we always recommend confirming coverage directly with your carrier.
Mold can begin growing within 24–48 hours in warm, humid conditions — and South Jersey summers speed that up considerably. Even a winter pipe burst can leave hidden moisture behind walls that turns into a mold problem once the heat kicks back on. That's why fast extraction and structural drying matter more than almost anything else in the first two days after a loss.
