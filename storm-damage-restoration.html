Why It Happens

What Causes Storm Damage in South Jersey & Philadelphia

Nor'easters

Sustained wind and heavy rain from Nor'easters are one of the region's most common sources of roof and window damage.

Summer Thunderstorms

Fast-moving severe storms can bring damaging wind gusts and hail with very little warning.

Falling Trees & Limbs

Mature tree canopy in older Camden County neighborhoods means storm winds regularly bring down limbs onto roofs and siding.

Flash Flooding

Low-lying areas near the Delaware River and its tributaries are prone to fast flooding during heavy rain events.

Ice & Snow Load

Heavy, wet snow and ice buildup can stress roofs and gutters during winter storms.

Power Outages

Extended outages during major storms can lead to sump pump failure and secondary water damage.
