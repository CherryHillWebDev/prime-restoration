Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship, Ready When You Need Us

Prime Restoration has spent more than 20 years restoring homes and businesses across South Jersey and the greater Philadelphia area — handling everything from a single burst pipe to a full storm-damaged rebuild.

Our approach is simple: one team manages your project from the first emergency call through the final coat of paint. That means no juggling separate contractors for mitigation, demolition, and rebuild — just a single point of contact who already knows your project inside and out.

Disasters don't wait for business hours, so neither do we. Our office is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and we're licensed and insured for every job we take on, whether it's an emergency dispatch or a planned kitchen remodel.
