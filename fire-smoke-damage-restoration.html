Why It Happens

Common Causes We See Across South Jersey & Philadelphia

Kitchen Fires

Unattended cooking is one of the most frequent causes of residential fires and smoke damage.

Electrical Fires

Older wiring, common in many Camden County homes, can be a hidden fire risk behind walls.

Space Heaters

Portable heaters used during cold South Jersey winters are a common ignition source when placed too close to combustibles.

Appliance Malfunctions

Dryers, HVAC units, and other appliances can overheat and spark a fire without warning.

Lightning & Storms

Summer thunderstorms occasionally cause electrical fires from lightning strikes or power surges.

Chimney & Fireplace Fires

Built-up creosote in older homes' chimneys is a common cold-weather fire risk.

Good To Know

Not All Smoke Damage Is the Same

How we clean depends heavily on what burned — different fires leave behind different residue.

Wet Smoke

Low-Heat, Smoldering Fires

Leaves a sticky, smeary residue with a strong odor. Common with furniture or foam-based fires.

Dry Smoke

Fast, High-Heat Fires

Leaves a fine, powdery soot that's easier to wipe away but spreads into small cracks and crevices.

Protein Smoke

Kitchen & Cooking Fires

Often nearly invisible but leaves a strong odor and a greasy film, especially on kitchen surfaces.
